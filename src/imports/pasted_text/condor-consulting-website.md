Design a high-end, modern consulting website for **Condor Consulting Group** with the aesthetic quality of elite strategy and defense-technology firms.

Design inspiration:
Palantir, Anduril, McKinsey Digital, BCG X, Stripe, and modern venture-backed technology firms.

The design should feel:
confident, strategic, minimal, and authoritative.

Color palette (strict):
• Condor red (#C8102E style tone)
• Slightly off-black background (#0F1113 range)
• White / off-white typography
• Subtle gray interface elements

Avoid bright colors or gradients other than subtle red accents.

Typography:
Modern clean sans-serif similar to Inter, Helvetica Now, or Söhne.

Design style:
• dark mode primary layout
• very strong grid structure
• large confident typography
• generous whitespace
• subtle red accents and thin dividing lines
• minimal icons
• no corporate stock photos
• abstract or technical graphic elements if needed

The design must feel credible for government agencies, defense contractors, and institutional clients.

The layout should export cleanly to HTML/CSS suitable for static hosting (Cloudflare Pages).
